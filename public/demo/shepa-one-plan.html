1. What I saw

  • The App Store page doesn't show a rating score yet ("not enough ratings or reviews"). For a social app, that is often where people decide not to install.
  • In a language-exchange app, a new user who waits and sees nobody usually leaves. Your AI Talk can fill that gap.
  • Three small store details worth checking: a "month $299.00" in-app purchase next to "vip_month $4.99"; a 4+ age rating for an app with video calls with strangers; and a Chinese full-width comma in the description.

2. Step 1: ratings from happy users · $149 fixed

The App Store rating prompt shows only when all of these are true:

  1. The conversation lasted 3 minutes or more.
  2. It is not the user's first session.
  3. We haven't asked in the last 120 days.
  4. No report and no connection problem in that call.

After a call, the user answers "How was your conversation?" privately. "Not good" opens a private feedback form that goes to you, with the session details. On iOS this uses Apple's own review prompt.

Android: Google's guidelines say no questions may be asked before or while showing the in-app review card. So on Android the review card is triggered by the objective rules above only, and the "How was it?" question stays separate.

Delivery: 2 to 3 working days after I get access. You pay when it works in your TestFlight build.

3. Step 2: talking within 60 seconds · $395 fixed

  • Only 2 questions before the first call: what you speak, and what you want to practise.
  • Match for up to 20 seconds. If nobody fits, offer AI Talk straight away and notify the user when a person comes online.
  • A starter topic on screen, so nobody freezes.
  • Profile, photo and follows come after the first good conversation, not before.

Delivery: about one week. Same terms. Or hourly, if you prefer: $15 an hour.
