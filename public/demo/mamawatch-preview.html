Pregnant? These signs mean: go to a health facility today.

  • Severe headache that does not go away
  • Blurred vision or seeing spots
  • Sudden swelling of the face, hands or feet
  • Pain high in the belly, under the ribs
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Fits or convulsions: this is an emergency

Do not wait. These are not malaria or stress until a health worker has checked.

If you see one of these signs:
  1. Go to the nearest hospital or health centre now, even at night.
  2. Bring your antenatal (ANC) card.
  3. Ask someone to go with you. Do not travel alone.

MamaWatch Watchers help you recognise danger signs and reach care. They do not make a diagnosis.

Why MamaWatch exists

Stella's headaches, swelling and fatigue were dismissed as malaria or stress. A neighbour recognised the danger signs and insisted she return to hospital. That neighbour saved her life. MamaWatch trains communities so every mother has that neighbour.

How MamaWatch works

Recognize early

Women, families and communities learn the pregnancy danger signs and when to seek care.

Speak up and act early

Take worrying symptoms seriously and go for care without delay.

Community Watchers

Trusted community members watch over mothers and encourage timely referral, without replacing health workers.

Extend learning

Survivor stories, community education, digital engagement and peer support.
